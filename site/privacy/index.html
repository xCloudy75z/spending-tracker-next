Privacy model

Your records stay with you.

Spending Tracker is deliberately local-first. It has no account system, advertising SDK, analytics tracker, or application server.

The app uses local-only browser storage. Your transactions, plan, settings, and card details stay on this device unless you explicitly export a file.

What is stored

The app stores the entries you create, planning values, card records, language and theme preferences, and a small schema version used to keep the data readable across app updates.

What leaves your device

The application does not transmit your spending data. Opening the hosted app downloads static HTML, CSS, JavaScript, icons, and other interface assets from GitHub Pages. Your financial records are not part of those requests.

Backups are your responsibility

Local-only storage is private, but it cannot rescue data after Safari website data is cleared, a device is lost, or the app is removed. Use the app’s JSON backup regularly. Store the file in a secure location you control and test the restore flow before depending on it.

Shared devices

Anyone with access to your unlocked browser profile may be able to open the app and view its locally stored data. The app does not add its own password or encryption layer.

Deleting your data

You can remove individual records in the app. To remove everything, clear this site’s website data in the browser. Export a backup first if you may need the records again.

Security boundaries

The app uses a restrictive content security policy and has no external runtime scripts. Device security, browser security, backup-file handling, and physical access remain outside the app’s control.

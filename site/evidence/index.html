Offline verification detail

A cold offline reload is exercised in desktop Chromium after a successful online visit. On Windows, Playwright’s WebKit engine does not provide the same context-level offline switch; the WebKit runs instead verify the cached shell and modules, service-worker registration, offline event handling, and every application route. This limitation is stated here so the evidence is not stronger than the test.

Small, focused, useful

A spending plan you can actually use daily.

The app starts with one practical answer—what is safe today—then keeps planning, cards, activity, and portability close at hand.

01

Today

See your safe-to-spend amount, cycle progress, unallocated balance, and recent activity without digging through reports.

02

Quick capture

Add spending or income in a short, keyboard-friendly dialog with date, note, category, and payment source.

03

Activity

Search and filter transactions, review running totals, edit mistakes, and delete records with a clear confirmation.

04

Plan

Set the amount available to spend, preferred cycle-start day, savings target and treatment, category budgets, and the next rollover.

05

Cards

Keep outstanding card purchases, paid items, and wife reimbursements visible without mixing those obligations into cash spending.

06

SMS paste

Paste a bank message and let the app suggest the amount, merchant, and transaction type before you confirm.

07

Backup and restore

Download a complete JSON backup, validate it before restore, and export transactions to CSV for your own archive.

08

English and Arabic

Switch language in place. Arabic includes a genuine right-to-left layout rather than translated labels in a left-to-right shell.

09

Offline PWA

After the first successful visit, the app shell is cached for use without a connection and can be added to an iPhone home screen.

No hidden dependency

No account. No database server. No subscription.

The app is static and stores financial records only in your browser on your device.
